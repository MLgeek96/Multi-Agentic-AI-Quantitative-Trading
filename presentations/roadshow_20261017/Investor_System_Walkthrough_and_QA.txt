5 EXPLAIN THE TRADING MODEL
SHOW: Pairs model diagnostics with the Z score hedge beta formation gate and OU projection.
PRESENTER: Pairs PM owner

SAY:
Pairs trading tests whether a temporary departure in two assets' relationship may revert. The Kalman filter updates the estimated hedge relationship between log prices. The OU model describes possible mean reversion in the residual spread and produces forecasts at several session horizons.

The default entry threshold is an absolute Z score of one point two five, the exit band is zero point one, and the divergence stop is three point five. The adapter blocks a new position at extreme divergence or when the OU model is invalid. Frozen formation history through December 2024 must qualify before a new research entry.

A confirmed open position also has a time limit. We estimate its OU half life using information available on its opening session, multiply that by two point five, round up, and cap it at forty five sessions. Later model estimates cannot extend that original time limit.

The forecast is a model projection, not an observed return. The estimated relationship can break, and a pair can carry market, sector, liquidity, financing, and borrow risks.


6 EXPLAIN INDEPENDENT FUND RISK
SHOW: Risk and protection then Approved targets and risk outcomes.
PRESENTER: Risk owner

SAY:
A statistical hedge is still an exposure that needs a capital limit. Fund risk caps total gross exposure at eighty percent of marked fund value and each symbol at twenty percent. It counts absolute long and short amounts before any netting across pair groups.

It also checks the smaller of the Pairs mandate and its current marked equity, with a two percent collateral buffer and a fifteen basis point fee reserve. Short sale proceeds cannot enlarge the collateral mandate.

When a limit binds, one common factor scales both actionable legs. A three hundred thousand dollar long paired with a four hundred and fifty thousand dollar short becomes one hundred and eighty thousand and two hundred and seventy thousand at a sixty percent scale. The signed dollar hedge ratio remains connected.

At twelve percent drawdown the standard gross ceiling falls from eighty to forty percent. At twenty five percent, the persistent breaker requests exits on eligible sessions. These are target controls; they cannot guarantee a realized loss limit.

INVESTOR QUESTIONS AND SUGGESTED ANSWERS

1. Where does the return hypothesis come from?
From potential mean reversion in the relative price spread, modeled with a Kalman hedge relationship and OU process. This hypothesis needs forward validation after costs. The AI evidence layer has no measured investment return contribution.

2. Why did you remove Crypto?
The current mandate concentrates on the teammate's Pairs strategy. The Crypto PM and its allocation are removed from the active workflow; its former allocation is central cash. Earlier crypto research is retained as historical work.

3. Is this exactly the teammate strategy?
The four numerical modules are pinned and attributed. The fund adapter adds frozen formation gating without an unqualified fallback, signed linked sizing, session validation, independent fund risk, and a holding cap using the opening-session OU half life. It corrects residual forecast standardization. It does not import upstream backtest returns as fund performance.

4. Is there a new upstream revision?
The remote inspected on 17 October still points to 5d42b04. Those numerical engines were already imported. This revision changes their active fund integration and removes Crypto; it does not claim a new upstream commit.

5. Why only Visa and Mastercard?
They form the current declared US equity mandate and are checked on frozen formation history. Other upstream candidates are not automatically selected from their later returns. Additional pairs need explicit configuration, data, calendars, currency conversions, and validation.

6. Are the pairs market neutral?
The signed estimated dollar hedge ratio is preserved through risk scaling. That does not guarantee neutrality to all market or sector risks. Negative beta can even produce same-sign target legs; the record retains that sign rather than forcing a conventional long and short.

7. How is the holding limit determined?
Using only history through the confirmed opening session, the adapter estimates OU half life and takes the rounded-up product with 2.5, capped at 45 sessions. An invalid entry estimate falls back to the hard cap. It records the estimate and effective limit.

8. Does the breaker guarantee a maximum loss?
No. At the threshold it requests exits and stays latched across restarts. Prices, liquidity, closed markets, and future execution determine realized losses. This prototype records the exit request without fills.

9. Does the soft control halve every position?
It halves the standard fund gross ceiling from 80 to 40 percent. Targets already below that ceiling may be unchanged. Other limits can bind first, and confirmed closed-market holdings retain their units.

10. What happens if an AI explanation is wrong?
Digest and structured metric mismatches are rejected. Free text still requires review. Because replies are handled after quantitative risk, they cannot change targets or place orders.

11. Did a language model run in the default demo?
No. The tools and computed memos run deterministically. Optional imported model replies have a separate status. Automatic provider calls are not connected.

12. Is the Phantom Book a simulated return portfolio?
It currently compares submitted and approved gross exposure. It does not simulate rejected fills or returns. Exposure removed is not profit or money saved.

13. What does the historical replay establish?
It establishes a reproducible cycle on the supplied historical close matrix, including qualification, signals, and risk. The 28 August replay stays flat. It does not establish live October history or a consolidated performance record.

14. Can investors use this remotely now?
The current portal is a localhost report reader with working navigation, evidence selection, tours, and exports. Investor authentication and a hosted deployment are not implemented.

15. What is required before live capital?
Reconciled fills and account accounting, borrow and financing treatment, executable quotes and lot sizes, linked-leg failure handling, access controls, reliable operations, and credible forward results after costs. Those milestones precede a commercial allocation case.
